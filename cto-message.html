Systems Architecture & Engineering Standards

Low Latency, Mechanical Sympathy & Scalable Topologies.

A technical breakdown from our Chief Technology Officer on how we engineer distributed architectures, maintain sub-50ms TTFB edge caching, and build resilient platforms designed for real-world concurrency.

Nurul Afsar Polash

Chief Technology Officer & Lead Systems Architect
Blood: AB+ polash@emythmakers.com
"True engineering beauty lies in low latency, self-healing topologies, deterministic deployments, and systems that scale gracefully without human panic."

At eMMSOFT, our architectural philosophy is rooted in pragmatism, measurable performance, and mechanical sympathy. We design software architectures that respect hardware constraints, eliminate network hops, and eradicate single points of failure before code ever reaches production.

When building digital platforms that must withstand millions of concurrent visitors, hope is not a strategy. You cannot optimize an ill-conceived architecture simply by throwing expensive cloud servers at it. Scalability must be baked into the foundational schema design, caching tiers, and data ingestion pipelines.

The Sub-50ms TTFB Benchmark for Newsroom CMS

Nowhere is system stress more unforgiving than in digital journalism. When national breaking news occurs, traffic does not ramp up gradually—it spikes by 500% to 2,000% in a span of ninety seconds.

Traditional monolithic content management systems collapse under these sudden surges because every page request triggers database joins and CPU-heavy template rendering. To solve this, our team engineered a purpose-built headless newsroom architecture:

  • Multi-Tiered Cache Topologies: We combine in-memory Redis cluster caching with Cloudflare Enterprise edge workers, ensuring that 99%+ of reader hits are resolved at the closest global edge node without touching origin databases.
  • Sub-150ms Edge Purging: When editors update a headline or publish breaking alerts, automated cache invalidation purges edge nodes nationwide in less than 150 milliseconds.
  • Live Collaborative Desks: Real-time WebSocket synchronization keeps active newsroom reporters, desk editors, and copywriters in sync without database lock contentions.

Resilient E-Commerce Architecture

In enterprise retail and multi-vendor marketplaces, our focus pivots to transactional determinism and inventory synchronization. Our e-commerce engines decouple the storefront presentation from the core transactional checkout and ERP synchronization layers.

This guarantees that even during intense flash sales, shopping carts never desynchronize, stock counts remain perfectly ACID-compliant, and checkout flows remain sub-second across mobile web and native apps.

Our Curated Technology Stack

We intentionally select technologies that excel in high-throughput, low-latency production environments:

  • Core Services: High-throughput Go and Node.js microservices with strict asynchronous execution boundaries.
  • Database Layer: ACID-compliant PostgreSQL schemas with optimized query plans, read-replicas, and connection poolers (PgBouncer).
  • In-Memory Layer: Distributed Redis clusters for session storage, live rate-limiting, and microsecond caching.
  • Infrastructure & DevOps: Containerized Docker & Kubernetes deployments with automated CI/CD pipelines and zero-downtime rolling updates.

A Commitment to Code Craftsmanship

Every module built in our studio undergoes peer architectural reviews, end-to-end regression testing, and rigorous load profiling before deployment. We treat software engineering as an exacting discipline. When your business grows, your digital foundation will scale with you seamlessly.

Nurul Afsar Polash
Chief Technology Officer & Lead Systems Architect, eMMSOFT
