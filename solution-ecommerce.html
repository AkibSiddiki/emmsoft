Commerce Architecture

Engineered for deterministic, race-condition-free scale.

We separate the ultra-fast headless mobile storefront from the transactional checkout and inventory reservation engine to guarantee no customer ever buys out-of-stock items during high-traffic drops.

Step 01

Headless Storefront

Next.js edge-rendered storefront with sub-second instant product filtering, server-side caching, and offline-ready cart states.

Step 02

Distributed Cart Gateway

Microservices handle real-time coupon validation, dynamic tax rates, localized shipping tiers, and multi-currency conversions.

Step 03

Redis Inventory Mutex

Atomic stock reservation locks prevent concurrent overselling while consumers proceed through the checkout funnel.

Step 04

ERP & Courier Webhooks

Automated two-way synchronization with warehouse ERP systems and automatic booking with major courier logistics dispatchers.

Multi-Warehouse & Physical POS Synchronization

Whether an item sells online at 2:00 AM or off a physical shelf in your flagship retail outlet, eMMSOFT's event bus immediately deducts inventory across all channels in real time, preventing reconciliation discrepancies and logistics headaches.

Execution Methodology

From inventory architecture to high-converting launch.

A rigorous 4-step engineering process built to guarantee zero transactional race conditions.

1

Catalog & ERP Schema Audit

We analyze existing product SKU taxonomies, multi-warehouse locations, tax rules, and POS integration touchpoints.

2

Headless Core & Mutex Build

We develop decoupled Next.js storefronts, configure Redis atomic reservation locks, and integrate localized payment gateways.

3

50k Concurrent Load Testing

We stress-test checkout endpoints with 50,000 simulated simultaneous buyers to verify zero overselling and seamless database failover.

4

Production Launch & Standby

Zero-downtime deployment backed by automated courier dispatch webhooks and 24/7 dedicated engineering standby during midnight flash sales.
