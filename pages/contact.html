Proposal FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding project onboarding, timeline estimation, and team allocation.

How quickly can eMMSOFT kick off a project?

Upon reviewing your inquiry and conducting an initial architecture discovery call (typically within 24–48 hours), we can assign a dedicated engineering squad within 3 business days.

Do you sign NDAs before initial discussions?

Yes. We treat all business models, technical assets, and client data with extreme confidentiality. We routinely sign non-disclosure agreements prior to reviewing project codebases or wireframes.

Can you modernize or rescue an existing codebase?

Absolutely. We specialize in high-concurrency re-architecting, migrating legacy monoliths to headless microservices, and optimizing slow database queries without disrupting live production traffic.

What support model do you provide post-launch?

We provide comprehensive 24/7 SLA monitoring, automated security patch updates, performance auditing, and ongoing feature enhancement retainers tailored to your platform's operational scale.
